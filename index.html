ConeVision

A traffic cone detector that runs in real time on a laptop CPU. I fine-tuned YOLOv8n in PyTorch, exported it to ONNX, quantized it to INT8, and wrote the detector in C++ on ONNX Runtime.

Train · PyTorch→Export · ONNX→Quantize · INT8→Deploy · C++→Drive · ROS 2 + Gazebo

The first INT8 model found nothing. YOLOv8 puts box coordinates (0–640) and scores (0–1) in one output, and a single 8-bit range rounded every score to zero. Quantizing only the convolution layers fixed it.

Invoice Review

A web app that reads supplier invoices with Azure AI, checks them against finance rules, and lets a person approve or reject them. The AI only extracts; rules decide, and a person makes the final call.

Azure OpenAIDocument IntelligenceFastAPIPydanticReact + TypeScriptDocker
143/143fields correct after merging, vs 133 with one model
13/13invoices flagged with exactly the expected issues
81tests, replaying recorded Azure responses offline

Why two readers. Document Intelligence missed the supplier's GST/HST number on 10 invoices. The OpenAI reading recovered every one, so ten valid invoices were not blocked. Measured on 14 generated sample documents in English and French.
